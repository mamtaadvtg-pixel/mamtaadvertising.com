FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do you handle SEO and paid ads together, or separately?

Together, as one plan — SEO builds long-term organic visibility while paid search and social fill the gaps in the near term. Running them separately usually means duplicated spend and inconsistent messaging.

What is GEO (Generative Engine Optimisation)?

GEO is the practice of structuring content so AI answer engines like ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity are more likely to cite your brand when someone asks a category question — a discipline we run alongside traditional SEO, not instead of it.

How long before we see results from SEO?

Meaningful organic movement typically takes 3–6 months depending on competition and starting point; paid channels can show results within days. We set expectations honestly at the planning stage, not after the invoice.

Do you build websites, or only market existing ones?

Both — we handle website and landing-page development where a campaign needs it, and we also optimise existing sites for conversion, speed, and search visibility.

Can digital be combined with our outdoor or broadcast campaign?

Yes — this is one of the most effective combinations we plan. Outdoor and broadcast build awareness at scale; digital captures and converts the demand that awareness creates.
