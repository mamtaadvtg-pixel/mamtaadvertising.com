FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How do you choose which influencers to work with?

By audience fit and engagement quality, not follower count alone — a smaller, highly engaged regional creator often outperforms a large but disengaged one for the same budget.

Do you manage celebrity endorsements as well as influencers?

Yes — from identification and negotiation through contracting and usage-rights management, celebrity endorsements are handled with the same rigour as influencer partnerships.

Do you handle contracts and usage rights?

Yes — content usage rights, exclusivity terms, and deliverable timelines are all negotiated and documented upfront, so there's no ambiguity once content goes live.

How is influencer campaign performance measured?

Against agreed metrics — reach, engagement rate, click-throughs, or direct sales via trackable links and codes — reported alongside the rest of your campaign data, not in isolation.

Do you work with regional-language creators?

Yes — Gujarati and other regional-language creators are often more effective than national English-language influencers for locally-targeted campaigns, and we maintain relationships across both.
