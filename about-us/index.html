Origin & Philosophy

Media that works together, not in silos

For more than 20 years, Mamta Advertising has grown alongside India's evolving media landscape — not by specialising in a single channel, but by mastering how channels work together. Our origin is rooted in traditional outdoor and print media, where strategic placement and negotiation on direct media rates built the foundation of client trust.

As media consumption shifted, that same principle — put the message in front of the right audience, at the right price, with measurable intent — extended naturally into radio, television, cinema, and now digital and performance marketing.

We don't think of ourselves as a vendor for a single format. We think of ourselves as a planning partner: the team a brand calls when a campaign needs to move from a hoarding on a highway to a paid social feed without losing a consistent strategy in between.

Independent by design: we don't own locked-in media inventory, which means every recommendation — a hoarding, a cinema screen, a transit display — is chosen because it fits the campaign, not because we need to sell it. Strategy is decided before a single media booking is made, never the other way around.

That independence is founder-led: every client works with direct strategic oversight from the top, backed by pan-India vendor relationships built over 20+ years — the kind of access and pricing that comes only from two decades of consistent, transparent dealing with media owners across the country.

How We Work

Corporate integrity & transparent dealing

We're known among the media houses, print publications, TV and radio networks, and hoarding owners we work with for one thing above all: straightforward, transparent dealing — we execute what we commit to, on the terms we agree to.

Budget estimates, campaign suggestions, and media plans we provide are built on real, current inventory availability and pricing — not inflated rate cards. Where a channel doesn't fit a client's objective, we say so, even if it costs us the booking.

In practice, this means
  • No commission structures hidden inside inflated media rates
  • Media availability confirmed directly with owners, not assumed
  • Strategy documented and agreed before any booking is made
  • Direct founder oversight on every active campaign
