Why Print Still Works

Four reasons it hasn't gone away

Inherited Credibility

Advertising in a respected publication borrows its readers' trust — especially valuable for healthcare, finance, and education categories.

A Considered Environment

Readers engage with print without notifications, tabs, or an ad-block reflex — the message gets a fair read.

Edition-Level Targeting

Regional editions let you target by city or district precisely, without paying for reach you don't need.

Longer Shelf Life

Magazines and supplements sit in homes and waiting rooms for weeks — a digital impression is gone in seconds.

Planned Alongside Digital

Print builds trust. Digital builds action.

We rarely recommend print in isolation. A newspaper campaign that establishes credibility pairs naturally with a digital push that captures the search and social attention it generates — planned as one system, not two separate bookings.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does print advertising still work in 2026?

Yes — particularly for credibility-sensitive categories like healthcare, finance, and real estate, and for reaching readers who engage deeply with content rather than scanning a feed.

Should I advertise in Gujarati or English newspapers?

Gujarati publications typically suit regional consumer campaigns with strong local trust; English suits executive and premium audiences. Many campaigns combine both for full coverage — we recommend the mix based on your specific audience.

Do you handle ad design and production?

Yes — we coordinate ad design, layout, and print-ready artwork to each publication's exact specifications, so there's no back-and-forth with the publisher on your end.

How is print advertising priced?

Pricing depends on publication, edition, page position, size, and duration. We don't use fixed rate cards — every plan is priced against your specific brief.

Can print be combined with our digital campaign?

Yes — print builds credibility and reach while digital converts and measures. We plan both together as one system wherever it strengthens the campaign.
