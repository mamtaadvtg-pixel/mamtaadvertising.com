Regional vs National

Which television mix is right for you?

Regional Gujarati Channels

Best for consumer brands targeting Gujarat households — high local trust, cultural relevance, and competitive rates versus national buys.

National Hindi & English Channels

Best for pan-India brand-building or reaching business and affluent audiences beyond a single state.

Cinema, Either Way

A strong complement to both — premium context and full attention regardless of your primary channel strategy.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Should I advertise on regional or national television?

Regional Gujarati channels suit local consumer campaigns at competitive rates; national channels suit broader brand-building. Many campaigns combine both — we recommend the right mix based on your objective and budget.

Do you produce television commercials?

Yes — we coordinate creative concept, scripting, production, and post-production to broadcaster specifications, from a 10-second cutdown to a full 60-second film.

Is cinema advertising expensive?

Cinema campaigns scale by screen count, city, and duration — we recommend an approach that fits your budget and objective rather than a fixed package.

Can television be combined with digital video?

Yes — a connected-TV-plus-digital-video strategy reaches both broadcast and streaming audiences with consistent messaging and frequency.

How do you measure television effectiveness?

We use GRP (Gross Rating Points) planning alongside reach and frequency targets; for direct-response campaigns, we also track correlations with digital and sales data.
