FAQ

Common questions

How is mall advertising different from retail branding?

Mall advertising is paid media placement inside malls — hoardings, screens, kiosks, atrium takeovers. Retail branding covers in-store signage, navigation, and trade activation, handled under our Retail & Shopper Marketing service.

Can mall advertising be combined with outdoor or airport media?

Yes — mall branding is often combined with roadside outdoor and airport campaigns for a coordinated, multi-touchpoint approach reaching the same premium consumer.

Which malls do you cover in Gujarat and Mumbai?

We plan across leading organised retail destinations in Ahmedabad, Surat, Vadodara, Rajkot and Mumbai. The specific mall mix depends on your target audience, category and budget — confirm current availability with us directly, as mall inventory changes.

Do you handle experiential activations, or only media placement?

Both — kiosk activations, product sampling, and branded experiential zones are planned alongside standard atrium and digital media placements.
