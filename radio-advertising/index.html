Time-Band Planning

When your audience actually listens

Peak listenership — commuters, professionals, and families. The highest-impact window for most consumer brands.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Which FM station is best for my campaign?

There's no single answer — station selection depends on audience profile, city, language preference, and programme compatibility. We recommend based on your specific objective, not a fixed station preference.

What are RJ mentions?

Presenter-led promotional integrations delivered in the RJ's own conversational style — brand mentions, product discussions, and event announcements within programme content, subject to station policy.

Do you produce the radio commercials?

Yes — scriptwriting, voice coordination, recording, editing, and audio mastering to broadcaster specifications are all handled in-house or through our production partners.

How many spots should we run per day?

Frequency recommendations depend on campaign duration, budget, and awareness objective — we build a frequency plan as part of the overall media strategy.

Can radio be combined with outdoor advertising?

Yes — radio and outdoor together create a powerful frequency and recall combination for commuter audiences. We plan both as one coordinated campaign wherever it makes sense.
