By accessing our website, submitting an enquiry, or using our services, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy.

1. Scope

This Policy applies to visitors to our website, prospective and existing clients, business partners, media partners, vendors, and individuals who contact us through any channel — website, email, telephone, WhatsApp Business, or social media.

2. Information We Collect

Personal Information: name, company, designation, email, mobile number, business address.

Business Information: industry, marketing objectives, advertising requirements, campaign budgets, preferred media channels.

Website Usage: IP address, browser type, operating system, pages visited, time spent, referral source.

Cookies: essential, analytics, performance, and functional cookies. See Section 9 and our Cookie Policy.

3. How We Use Your Information

Responding to enquiries; preparing proposals and quotations; executing campaigns; sending service announcements (opt-out available); improving website performance; and complying with applicable law.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

We process information based on user consent, performance of a contract, legitimate business interests, or compliance with legal obligations — as applicable to each processing activity.

5. Information Sharing

Mamta Advertising does not sell personal information to third parties. Information may be shared with service providers (hosting, analytics), media partners (only information necessary for campaign execution), professional advisors, and government authorities where required by law.

6. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organisational safeguards including HTTPS encryption, access controls, and internal confidentiality practices. No method of internet transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Data Retention

Information is retained only as long as necessary to provide services, fulfil contractual obligations, meet legal requirements, and maintain business records.

8. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information. Contact us at mamtaadvtg@gmail.com to exercise these rights.

9. Cookies

We use essential, analytics, and functional cookies. You may disable cookies via browser settings; this may affect website functionality. See our Cookie Policy for full details.

10. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for businesses and professionals. We do not knowingly collect information from children.
