FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is airport advertising suitable for every business?

Not necessarily — it works best for premium, considered-purchase categories. We recommend it only when it genuinely aligns with your audience and campaign objective, not as a default upsell.

How much does airport advertising cost?

Pricing varies by airport, terminal, format, duration and location within the terminal. Every campaign receives a customised proposal rather than a fixed rate card.

Which airport should we advertise at — Ahmedabad, Surat, or Mumbai?

Ahmedabad suits broad Gujarat and international campaigns; Surat suits trade and business-community campaigns; Mumbai (CSMIA) suits national and premium brand campaigns reaching India's highest-traffic gateway. We recommend the right fit based on your audience and objective.

What is aerobridge branding?

Aerobridge branding places your creative on the walls of the enclosed bridge passengers walk through between the terminal and the aircraft — a close-proximity, distraction-free format with guaranteed exposure to every boarding passenger on that specific route.

Can airport advertising be combined with roadside outdoor?

Yes — airport plus roadside OOH creates consistent multi-touchpoint visibility for the same premium audience at different moments in their journey. See our full outdoor format library →
