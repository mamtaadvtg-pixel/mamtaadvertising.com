1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember user preferences, improve navigation, measure performance, and understand visitor behaviour.

2. Types of Cookies We Use

Essential Cookies — necessary for the website to function correctly. Without these, certain parts of the website may not operate properly.

Analytics Cookies — help us understand pages visited, time spent, and traffic sources in aggregated form to improve performance.

Performance Cookies — measure website speed and identify technical issues.

Functional Cookies — remember preferences such as light/dark theme settings.

3. Why We Use Cookies

To improve usability, monitor performance, analyse visitor behaviour, detect technical problems, and understand content effectiveness. We do not use cookies to sell your personal information.

4. Cookie Consent

Where applicable, visitors may be presented with a consent banner allowing acceptance, rejection, or customisation of non-essential cookies. Preferences may be updated at any time through browser settings.

5. Managing or Disabling Cookies

Most browsers allow you to view, delete, or block cookies. Instructions vary by browser — Chrome, Edge, Firefox, and Safari each publish their own guidance. Disabling essential cookies may affect certain website functions.

6. Third-Party Cookies

Some pages embed third-party services (Google Maps, Google Analytics) which may set their own cookies subject to their respective privacy policies.

7. Changes to This Policy
