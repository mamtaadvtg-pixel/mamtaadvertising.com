FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do you handle both PR and advertising together?

Yes — PR, advertising, and digital are planned under one roof so your public narrative and paid media stay consistent, rather than sending mixed messages.

Can you manage a full product launch event?

Yes — from concept and venue sourcing to stage design, guest management, and post-event media coverage, working closely with our Event Management team.

Do you support crisis communication on short notice?

Yes — crisis communication planning and media statement preparation are part of our standing services; actual response speed depends on the specific situation and how much groundwork is already in place.

Do you guarantee media coverage?

No — no ethical PR agency can guarantee coverage, since publication decisions rest with editors and journalists. What we guarantee is a well-crafted pitch, the right media relationships, and a professional process.

Do you work with government or institutional clients?

Yes — institutional and government communication is part of our regional expertise, particularly across Gujarat and Delhi.
