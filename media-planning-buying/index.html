FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do you only plan media, or also negotiate and buy it?

Both — we handle strategy and planning, then negotiate and buy directly with media owners across every channel, from outdoor hoardings to Google Ads.

Can you take over media buying for a campaign someone else created?

Yes — we regularly step in purely for planning, negotiation, and buying, working with creative developed elsewhere without needing to redo it.

Do you take agency commissions from media owners?

Any commission structures are disclosed transparently as part of our commercial proposal — we don't hide compensation inside inflated media rates. Ask us directly during scoping and we'll walk you through exactly how we're paid.

How do you stay unbiased if you also plan the strategy?

Because we don't own media inventory. We have no incentive to favour one channel or vendor over another — only the campaign outcome.

What size budgets do you work with?

We plan across a wide range — from single-city launches to national, multi-channel programmes. The process scales to the budget, not the other way around.
