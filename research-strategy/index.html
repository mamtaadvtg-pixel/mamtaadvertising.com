FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can you do research and strategy without also running the media campaign?

Yes — market research, data-driven strategy, and campaign effectiveness analysis are all available as standalone engagements, independent of media buying.

Do you analyse campaigns that other agencies planned?

Yes — campaign effectiveness analysis can be run independently of who planned or executed the original campaign, giving an outside view of what actually worked.

How long does the research phase take?

It depends on scope — a focused competitive scan can take a few days, while full market research and segmentation takes longer. We scope timelines upfront before committing to a date.

Do you use primary or secondary research?

Both, depending on budget and need — from desk research and existing data to primary surveys or interviews where the objective justifies the additional cost.

What deliverable do we get?

A written strategy document translating research findings into a recommended channel mix and messaging approach — something your team can act on directly, not a slide deck full of raw data.
