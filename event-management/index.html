FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do you handle events end-to-end, or just venue coordination?

End-to-end — concept, venue sourcing, vendor management, guest list coordination, stage/set design, and on-ground execution, through to post-event reporting and media follow-up.

Can you manage a multi-city roadshow?

Yes — multi-city and multi-venue activations are coordinated centrally so branding, messaging, and quality stay consistent across every stop.

Do you coordinate media coverage for press meets and launches?

Yes — press meets and launches are typically planned alongside our PR team, so media invitations, coverage, and follow-up are handled as one coordinated effort.

How far in advance should we plan an event?

For a straightforward press meet, 3–4 weeks is workable; for a major product launch or multi-city roadshow, 8–12 weeks gives the best venue and vendor options.

Can event management be combined with our outdoor or digital campaign?

Yes — a launch event is often the centrepiece of a wider campaign, with outdoor, digital, and PR built around the event date rather than run separately.
