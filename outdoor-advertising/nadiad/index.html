FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is Nadiad served locally or through a partner network?

Because Nadiad is close to our Ahmedabad head office and sits directly on the NH48 corridor we already travel regularly, it's typically planned and inspected in person by our own team rather than through a distant execution partner.

Is Nadiad worth a standalone campaign, or only combined with other cities?

Both work — Nadiad's own district economy (manufacturing, retail, education) supports a standalone campaign, and its highway frontage makes it a strong low-cost addition to a wider Ahmedabad–Vadodara plan.

What formats are available on the NH48 stretch through Nadiad?

Primarily large-format hoardings and unipoles suited to highway visibility and speed; exact availability is confirmed at the planning stage.

Can Nadiad be combined with Anand and Vadodara?

Yes — this is a common and cost-efficient way to plan the wider Charotar/Kheda district as one regional campaign rather than three separate bookings.
