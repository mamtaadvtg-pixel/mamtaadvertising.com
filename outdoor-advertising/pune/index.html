FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do you have a local office in Pune?

Not a dedicated branch office — Pune campaigns are planned by our core team and executed through vetted on-ground partners, with a single Mamta point of contact throughout.

Can you plan both IT-corridor and industrial-belt campaigns in Pune?

Yes — Hinjewadi's IT audience and the Chakan/Pune–Mumbai Expressway industrial audience need different corridors and formats, and we plan them as two distinct briefs when a campaign needs both.

What's the typical lead time for a Pune campaign?

Similar to our HQ markets — roughly 3–5 weeks from brief to live, depending on format and site availability.

Can Pune be combined with our Mumbai or Gujarat activity?

Yes — this is a common request, and it's exactly where a single planning desk across markets pays off, both in consistent creative and in negotiating leverage.
