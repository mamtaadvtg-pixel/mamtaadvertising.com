Why Outdoor Still Wins

Digital gets skipped. A hoarding on your commute cannot be.

Outdoor advertising remains the only medium that builds unavoidable, repeated physical presence in the exact geography your customers already move through — every single day, whether or not they open an app.

Physical Presence

A hoarding a commuter passes twice daily for three months builds a consistent mental anchor no skippable ad can replicate.

Contextual Scale

A building wrap or a unipole at a city entry point signals investment and credibility that a banner ad simply cannot.

Unavoidable Reach

You cannot retarget an audience that has never heard of you. Outdoor builds the awareness digital later converts.

Local Precision

Corridor-level planning — not city-wide averages — means your budget lands on the roads your actual buyers use.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Which Indian cities does Mamta Advertising cover for outdoor advertising?

We operate at full local depth in six HQ markets — Ahmedabad, Surat, Vadodara, Rajkot, Gandhinagar and Mumbai — and run active, expanding campaigns in Bangalore, Pune, Hyderabad, Delhi, Anand and Nadiad through our pan-India planning network, plus project work across many more cities.

What outdoor advertising formats do you plan and buy?

Hoardings and billboards, unipoles and gantries, Digital Out-of-Home (DOOH), bus shelter and transit media, metro branding, airport advertising, mall and retail media, and building or highway wraps — selected on audience fit, not on which vendor has space free.

How is outdoor advertising priced?

Pricing depends on format, location, size, duration and seasonal demand. We don't use fixed rate cards — every plan is built around your specific audience and objective, then priced accordingly.

Can outdoor be combined with digital or broadcast?

Yes — outdoor builds unavoidable physical presence and reach; digital and broadcast add targeting, frequency and conversion. We plan all three as one system rather than separate bookings.

How far in advance should we plan a campaign?

Prime corridors in HQ markets typically book 4–6 weeks ahead, especially around festive seasons. Earlier planning gives more choice of location and format.
