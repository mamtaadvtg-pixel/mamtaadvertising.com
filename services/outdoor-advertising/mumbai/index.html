Key Corridors

Where Mumbai's audience moves

Mumbai's commercial geography breaks into a small number of distinct corridors — each with its own traffic pattern, audience profile, and advertising opportunity. We plan against the corridor, not the city average.

CORRIDOR 01

Western Express Highway

Mumbai's highest-traffic commuter artery, running from Bandra to Borivali — unmatched frequency for mass-reach brand campaigns.

High TrafficMass Reach
CORRIDOR 02

Bandra–Kurla Complex (BKC)

Mumbai's premier financial and corporate district — banks, consulates, and Grade-A offices. The strongest corridor for B2B and premium brand campaigns.

CorporateB2BPremium
CORRIDOR 03

Linking Road & Bandra

High-footfall retail and lifestyle corridor — fashion, F&B, and consumer brands reaching an affluent, trend-forward audience.

RetailLifestyle
CORRIDOR 04

Eastern Express Highway

Connects the eastern suburbs and industrial belt — strong for manufacturing, logistics, and value-conscious consumer brands.

IndustrialValue

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does Mamta Advertising have a real local presence in Mumbai, or is this managed remotely from Ahmedabad?

We have a dedicated branch office at Marine Lines, Mumbai — site visits, vendor negotiation, and campaign management are handled locally, coordinated with our Ahmedabad head office for national campaigns.

How does Mumbai pricing compare to Gujarat markets?

Mumbai is India's most competitive advertising market, and premium corridors like BKC and Western Express Highway command accordingly higher rates. We build a plan around your budget rather than assuming you need the most expensive sites.

Can you plan a campaign across Mumbai and Gujarat together?

Yes — this is one of the advantages of having both a head office in Ahmedabad and a branch office in Mumbai. National and regional campaigns spanning both markets are planned as one strategy, not two separate bookings.

How far in advance should we book Mumbai outdoor media?

Prime Mumbai corridors, especially BKC and Western Express Highway, can book 6–8 weeks ahead during festive and awards-season demand. Earlier planning secures better placement and rates.

Do you handle metro and suburban rail branding in Mumbai?

Yes — station branding, train wraps, and platform media across Mumbai's expanding metro lines and suburban rail network are part of our Mumbai format library.
